# Arena Brasil

## What it is

Arena Brasil is an offline football companion for people who want to explore a demonstration Brazilian football season. It includes sample fixtures, results, clubs, tables, players and statistics; it does not provide live scores or a real schedule.

## Launch and onboarding

A cold launch opens the “Home” tab, titled “Arena Brasil”. There is no onboarding or sign-in.

The screen begins with “THE BEAUTIFUL GAME” and “Your football. Your Brasil.”, followed by a “MATCH SPOTLIGHT” card marked “DEMO”. It shows Flamengo versus Palmeiras at “21:30”, “13 NOV · BRT”, “Brasileirão · Round 6” and “Maracanã · Rio de Janeiro”. Tapping “Open match centre”, or the card, opens the match.

Below are “Search teams or competitions”, competition filters, “Around the grounds”, upcoming sample matches and the four tabs: “Home”, “Matches”, “Competitions” and “My Football”.

## Screens

### “Home”

- “Search teams or competitions” filters upcoming matches by team or competition. “Clear search” clears the text.
- “All”, “Brasileirão”, “Copa do Brasil” and “Libertadores” filter the list by competition.
- The star control, labelled “Filter favourite teams” for accessibility, limits the list to followed teams. Followed teams’ matches otherwise appear first.
- “Around the grounds” changes to “Picked for you” after a team is followed. Tapping a match opens “Match centre”.
- The spotlight is hidden while search text is entered.

### “Matches”

“November 2026” and “Demo schedule · BRT” introduce a date strip from 7 to 16 November. Tapping a date selects its fixtures. “All”, “In progress” and “Favourites” filter them; “Matchday” shows the result count. Tapping a match opens “Match centre”.

### “Competitions”

The screen says “The heart of Brazilian football.” and “Explore the tables, follow every round and discover the names on the scoresheet.”

Tapping “Brasileirão”, “Copa do Brasil” or “Libertadores” opens that competition. The first is labelled “League · 6 demo clubs”; the others are labelled “Cup · sample fixtures”. Each shows “2026 SEASON” and a star when followed.

### Competition detail

The title is the selected competition, with “2026 · DEMO SEASON”. The star, labelled “Follow competition” or “Unfollow competition” for accessibility, toggles following.

- “Table” shows “League table” and “After 5 sample rounds” for “Brasileirão”. A club row opens its team profile. The table uses “CLUB”, “P”, “GD” and “PTS”, with an explanation of points and tie-breaking beneath it.
- “Fixtures” shows a “Round” menu and match rows. Select “Round 1” through “Round 7” for “Brasileirão”; the cups offer “Round 1”. A match opens “Match centre”.
- “Scorers” shows “Top scorers”, “Illustrative players” and goal totals from completed sample matches.
- On a cup’s “Table”, “Show fixtures” switches to “Fixtures”.

### “Match centre”

The match header shows the competition and round, teams, score or scheduled time, status, date, BRT time and stadium, with “DEMONSTRATION MATCH”. Tapping either team opens its profile.

- “Save match” saves the fixture in “My Football”; “Saved match” removes it.
- The bell, labelled “Demo reminder” for accessibility, opens a “Demo reminder” choice. “Remind me in one minute” requests notification permission if needed and schedules a local demonstration notification. “Cancel reminder” removes a pending reminder; “Not now” closes the choice.
- The “Form”, “Line-ups” and “Head-to-head” shortcuts select those sections. The section controls are “Events”, “Line-ups”, “Statistics”, “Head-to-head” and “Form”.
- “Events” shows “Match timeline” and “Illustrative events”. A scored sample has “Kick-off”, goals, and either “Full time” or “Sample paused”; the latter says “Frozen demonstration, not a live feed”.
- “Line-ups” shows “Sample line-ups”, “4–3–3” and “Illustrative players, not official team selections.”
- “Statistics” shows sample “Possession”, “Shots”, “Shots on target”, “Corners” and “Fouls” when a score is available, with “Sample match metrics for demonstration only.”
- “Head-to-head” shows “Previous meetings”; tapping a listed match opens it.
- “Form” shows “Team comparison”, “Recorded sample results”, form, “Points”, “Goals scored” and “Goals conceded”. The legend says “Brasileirão sample season · W win, D draw, L loss. Most recent first.”

### Team profile

The team name is the title; the header also shows its city. “Follow team” follows it, and “Following team” unfollows it.

- “Overview” shows “Last five matches”, “Season snapshot”, “Position”, “Played”, “Points” and “Next fixtures”. “View league table” opens “Brasileirão”; fixture rows open “Match centre”.
- “Fixtures” shows the team’s sample matches; tapping one opens it.
- “Squad” shows “Demonstration squad” and “Illustrative roster · not official club players”, with player numbers, names and roles.

### “My Football”

The screen says “Your side of the game.” and “A home for the clubs you love.”

- “My teams” shows the number following. “Manage” opens club selection and changes to “Done”; “Done” closes management. When no teams are followed, selection is shown automatically. “Find a team” filters the club list. Tap a club name to open its profile or its star to follow or unfollow it. Followed club rows also open profiles.
- “My competitions” lists all three competitions. Tap a competition name to open it or its star to follow or unfollow it.
- “Saved matches” lists saved fixtures; tapping one opens “Match centre”.
- “Reminders” lists pending demonstration reminders. “Cancel” removes one.
- “About this catalogue” shows “Works entirely offline”, “All matches, squads and statistics are included in the app. Favourites and reminders stay on this device.”, “Language” as “English (UK)”, and “Fixture time zone” as “Brasília · BRT”.

### “My football” widget

The small and medium widget is described as “Your saved football and the offline sample match.” It shows “Your football”, the number of teams followed and “Offline sample · not live scores”. The medium version also shows a saved match, or the spotlight match when none is saved. Tapping the widget opens “My Football”.

## Features

- Browse and search a “Built-in demo season · November 2026”.
- Explore “Match centre”, “Match timeline”, “Sample line-ups”, “Match statistics”, “Previous meetings” and “Team comparison”.
- Browse competition “Table”, “Fixtures” and “Scorers”.
- Follow teams and competitions, save matches and manage them in “My Football”.
- Set and cancel a one-minute “Demo reminder”.
- View followed-team progress and a sample match in the “My football” widget.
- Use the catalogue offline.

## Behaviours that can look like bugs

- The sample season is fixed. An “In progress” match says “Sample paused” and “Frozen demonstration, not a live feed”; its score and clock do not advance.
- “No matches found” says “Try another competition or add a favourite team in My Football.” Clear the search, change the competition, turn off the favourite filter or follow a team.
- “No fixtures here” says “Choose another date or filter to explore the sample season.” Select another date or filter.
- Cup “Table” shows “Cup format” and “This competition has no league table. Open Fixtures to explore the sample cup schedule.” Tap “Show fixtures” or “Fixtures”.
- “No fixtures in this round” says “Choose another round to view matches.” Select another “Round”.
- “No goals recorded” says “The sample competition has not started yet.” The cup scorer lists have no completed sample goals; “Brasileirão” has sample scorers.
- A scheduled match’s “Events” shows “Waiting for kick-off” and “The timeline will appear when events are available. Explore the team form and sample line-ups before the match.” The demonstration schedule does not advance; open a scored sample match to see events.
- A scheduled match’s “Statistics” shows “Statistics after kick-off” and “No match statistics are available for this scheduled fixture. Open Form to compare recorded sample results.” Open “Form” or a scored sample match.
- “No previous meetings” says “These teams have not met in the available sample catalogue.” Open another matchup to inspect its available history.
- “No fixtures available” says “There are no scheduled games for this team in the sample catalogue.”
- Before a match is saved, “Saved matches” shows “Keep a match close” and “Open any match and tap Save match. It will be waiting here next time.” Save a match to populate it.
- Before a reminder is set, “Reminders” says “No pending reminders. Open a match to try a one-minute demo reminder.” A reminder disappears from the pending list after it is delivered or cancelled.
- The reminder explains: “This is a sample fixture. A local demo reminder is sent one minute from now, rather than at a real kick-off time.” If permission is denied, the app says “Notifications are disabled. You can enable Arena Brasil notifications in Settings.”
- If saving fails, an “Arena Brasil” alert says “Your changes could not be saved. Please try again.” Its button is “OK”.

## Starter content and resume

The app starts with a built-in demonstration catalogue: six clubs—“Flamengo”, “Palmeiras”, “Corinthians”, “Santos”, “Fluminense” and “Botafogo”—and the three named competitions. It includes five completed sample league rounds, scheduled cup and league fixtures, one paused scored sample match, illustrative squads and statistics.

No teams, competitions or matches are followed or saved initially. Followed items and saved matches remain available on the device on a later launch; there is no unfinished editing workflow to resume.

## Permissions

Notifications are requested when the person chooses “Remind me in one minute” in “Demo reminder”. The app provides no custom notification usage-description string. The reminder is local and is scheduled for one minute later.

## Absent

Login or accounts, in-app purchases, ads, analytics, user-generated content, an account deletion flow and an App Tracking Transparency prompt are absent.

## Data and support

The catalogue is included in the app and works offline. Favourites, saved matches and reminders stay on this device. There is no on-screen Contact or Support control.

The catalogue note says “Built-in demo season · November 2026” and “Illustrative fixtures, players and statistics. Available entirely offline.” It identifies the source as “Arena Brasil sample catalogue” and displays an edition date.

## Scanning and health

None. The app does not scan barcodes or QR codes and does not show health, medical or product-health information.

## Platform

The interface uses English (UK), left-to-right layout and Brasília time, labelled “BRT”, regardless of device region. It supports iPhone and iPad on iOS 17.0 or later. iPhone supports portrait and both landscape orientations; iPad also supports upside-down portrait.
